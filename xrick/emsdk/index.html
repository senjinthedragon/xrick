Keys: arrows (or Z X O K) to move, Space to fire, P pause, E end game, Tab opens the settings menu (video, audio, controls, cheats, autoplay, quit -- settings are saved in the browser; not Escape, since that's the browser's own fullscreen-exit key). F4 mute, F5/F6 volume, F10/F11/F12 cycle upscaler/CRT shader/bezel. A connected gamepad works too (remap it from the menu). With no game chosen via the URL options below, you'll land on a carousel to pick Rick Dangerous or Rick Dangerous II; switching games, picking a world, or warping to a room are all settings-menu options from there, same as the native build. URL options: ?demo attract demo, ?speed=N, ?zoom=N, ?keys=L-R-U-D-F, ?nosound, and the menu's own ?game=/?map=/?submap= (for a direct link, skipping the carousel).
