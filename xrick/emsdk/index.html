Touch: on the diskette carousel, tap near an arrow (or swipe) to switch games, tap the middle to launch. In game, the screen is 9 tap zones (3 columns, rows 1/5-3/5-1/5): top-left jump, top-right down, mid-left/right move, bottom-left shoot, bottom-center fire, bottom-right bomb. Punch/stun (RD2) isn't its own zone: hold bottom-center together with mid-left or mid-right, same as the original game's fire+direction combo. Tap any settings-menu row to use it; tap anywhere to leave the credits page.

Arrows (or Z X O K) to move, Space to fire, P to pause, E to end the current game, Tab for the settings menu (video, audio, controls, cheats, autoplay).

F4 mute · F5/F6 volume · F10/F11/F12 cycle upscaler/CRT shader/bezel · a connected gamepad works too (remap it from the menu).

No game picked yet? Choose Rick Dangerous or Rick Dangerous II from the diskette carousel — picking a world or warping to a room are menu options too.
